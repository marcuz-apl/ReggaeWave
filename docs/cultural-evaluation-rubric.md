## 1. Principles of Cultural Integrity

Reggae is recognized by UNESCO as part of the **Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity**. It is a living, evolving musical, social, and spiritual practice originating in Jamaica—not merely a generic sound effect or tempo preset.

ReggaeWave upholds the following safeguards across all releases:
1. **Respect the Living Tradition**: Avoid caricature, exaggerated accents, cartoonish visual tropes, or superficial flags/colors as a substitute for musical substance.
2. **Zero Impersonation or Voice Cloning**: The creator's original lead vocal is preserved without alteration, cloning, or celebrity impersonation.
3. **Practitioner Review as Release Gate**: Every model, arrangement algorithm, and version release must be reviewed by qualified Reggae musicians and audio engineers before production sign-off.

---

## 2. Five-Dimension Evaluation Rubric

Each candidate output is evaluated on a **1 to 10 scale** across 5 core dimensions:

| Dimension | Description | Minimum Passing Score |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Rhythmic Authenticity** | Precision of the **One-Drop** (kick & rimshot on beat 3 only), **Steppers** (four-on-the-floor with beat 3 snare), and **Rockers** groove; natural swing and ghost notes without rigid robotic quantization. | **8.0 / 10** |
| **2. Melodic Sub-Bass & Space** | Presence of warm, rounded sub-bass (40–120 Hz fundamental); respect for classic Reggae "space" (leaving beat 1 open, melodic syncopation on beats 2 & 3, root-fifth walking pickups). | **8.5 / 10** |
| **3. Skank & Bubble Textures** | Rhythmic tightness of the offbeat guitar/piano chop (on the "&" of beats 2 & 4); fluidity of the Hammond organ bubble 16th-note rolls following harmonic chord transitions. | **8.0 / 10** |
| **4. Dub Space & Restraint** | Musicality of dynamic tape delay sends, feedback saturation, spring reverb splashes, and low-pass resonant filter sweeps without muddying the vocal or arrangement. | **8.0 / 10** |
| **5. Lead Vocal Integrity** | Clean separation of the original lead vocal melody, phrasing, dynamics, and emotional intent without robotic artifacts or pitch distortion. | **8.5 / 10** |

---

## 3. Evaluation Procedure

1. **Test Corpus**: The evaluation is conducted against the licensed test corpus spanning 5 distinct source genres (Pop, Rock, R&B/Soul, Electronic, Folk).
2. **Double-Blind A/B Listening**: Reviewers evaluate **Variation A (Classic Roots)** and **Variation B (Modern Steppers)** against reference productions.
3. **Release Sign-Off**: An arrangement release is approved only when the aggregate score across all dimensions exceeds **8.0 / 10.0** and zero dimensions score below 7.0.
